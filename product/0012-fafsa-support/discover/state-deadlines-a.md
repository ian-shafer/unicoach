# State FAFSA / state-aid deadlines — set A (AL AK AZ AR CA CO CT DE DC FL GA HI ID IL IN IA KS KY LA)

Checked on **2026-09-14**. 75 rows in
[`state-deadlines-a.csv`](state-deadlines-a.csv), covering 19 states and the
District of Columbia's own programs. 17 of the 19 have at least one row
confirmed from a state agency page fetched during this run. **AR and DE are not
verified at all** — see Gaps.

Award year is the start year: `2026` means the 2026-27 award year (FAFSA opened
24 September 2025). Regions and divisions are US Census Bureau.

## Method

Work was split between this agent (AL AK AZ AR CA CO CT DE DC FL GA) and one
sub-agent (HI ID IL IN IA KS KY LA). Both used the same method and the same
honesty rules, and both wrote into the schema above.

Search engines were not usable. The `websearch` skill has no API key; DuckDuckGo
HTML, its lite endpoint and Mojeek all returned a challenge page or a `202`. So
the method was: **guess the canonical state agency URL, fetch it directly** with
`httpx` and a browser User-Agent, strip tags, and grep the text for `deadline`,
month names, "as soon as possible", "until funds are exhausted" and "priority".
Then follow the `href`s on that page down to the individual program pages, which
is where the real text lives. PDFs (Florida fact sheets, the Kansas brochure,
the Louisiana administrative code) were parsed with `pypdf`.

studentaid.gov was **not** used. It is a JavaScript shell that returns no
content to a non-browser fetch, and the Wayback Machine has **no snapshot at
all** of its state-deadline page.

What blocked us, honestly:

- **`adhe.edu` and `sams.adhe.edu` (Arkansas)** are JavaScript applications. The
  served HTML contains three links and no deadline text. `scholarships.adhe.edu`
  resets the connection. Wayback's CDX index was returning "Internet Archive:
  Temporarily Offline" during this run, so the usual fallback was unavailable.
- **`education.delaware.gov` / `doe.k12.de.us` (Delaware)** return HTTP 403 to
  every fetch tried, including a full Chrome header set and a text-extraction
  proxy. Delaware has no Wayback snapshot for the higher-education pages either.
- **`isac.org` (Illinois)** returns 403 to a plain browser User-Agent but **200
  to a full Chrome header set** (`Accept`, `Accept-Language`, `Sec-Fetch-*`,
  `sec-ch-ua`, `Upgrade-Insecure-Requests`). That is the general trick for the
  Akamai/Cloudflare-fronted state sites; it did not work for Delaware.
- **`highered.az.gov` / `azgrants.az.gov` (Arizona)** serve the same homepage
  body for every path. The content is on `azregents.edu`, which is
  server-rendered.
- **`gafutures.org` (Georgia)** loads its "Application Procedure and Deadline"
  tab from a separate URL; fetching that sub-path directly gives the text.

Pages fetched, by state: `sp.ache.edu` (AL); `acpe.alaska.gov` (AK);
`azregents.edu` (AZ); `csac.ca.gov` (CA); `cdhe.colorado.gov` (CO);
`portal.ct.gov/ohe` (CT); `osse.dc.gov` (DC); `floridastudentfinancialaidsg.org`
fact-sheet and calendar PDFs (FL); `gafutures.org` program sub-pages (GA);
`hawaii.edu` and `uhcc.hawaii.edu` (HI); `nextsteps.idaho.gov` (ID); `isac.org`
(IL); `in.gov/che` and `learnmoreindiana.org` (IN); `educate.iowa.gov` (IA);
`kansasregents.gov` and its 2026-27 State Aid Brochure PDF (KS); `kheaa.com`
(KY); `mylosfa.la.gov` plus the LAC Title 28 Part IV rules PDF (LA).

One secondary source was used, and only where the agency was unreachable:
[savingforcollege.com "FAFSA Deadlines"](https://www.savingforcollege.com/article/fafsa-deadlines).
Every row from it is `source_tier = secondary` **and** `verified = unverified`.

## Hazards

Things that would mislead a family if copied straight onto a page.

- **DC is not a FAFSA deadline at all, and the secondary source has it wrong
  twice.** OSSE's own page says the DCTAG application — the **DC OneApp**, not
  the FAFSA — "will open on Feb. 2, 2026, and the application period will remain
  open until **Aug. 21, 2026, at 3 p.m.**" The OSSE page does not mention the
  FAFSA anywhere. The savingforcollege table gives DC as "June 25 / July 1".
  Neither of those dates appears on the agency page.
- **Florida publishes no statewide FAFSA date for its need-based grant, and the
  secondary source invents one.** The FSAG 2026-27 fact sheet says "Each
  participating institution determines application procedures, deadlines,
  student eligibility, and award amount." The savingforcollege table says
  Florida is "May 15". Do not show 15 May for Florida.
- **Florida's 1 April date is the FFAA, not the FAFSA.** The Florida Financial
  Aid Application is a separate state form, and 1 April is its deadline for four
  narrow programs (FFSS, CDDV, OCOE, Rosewood). Mixing the two forms is the
  single easiest way to mislead a Florida family.
- **Arizona's "priority dates" are the universities', not the state's.** ABOR
  lists ASU 15 January and NAU/UA 1 April. Those are institutional. The state
  program (Arizona Promise) is 1 April, and even that is worded as
  "encouraged... to receive priority consideration", not a cut-off.
- **The old Delaware outreach domain is now squatted.**
  `delawaregoestocollege.org` serves unrelated filler content about the US
  education system. It must never be cited.
- **California needs two things, not one.** A Cal Grant needs the FAFSA or CADAA
  **and** a verified GPA or test score on file with CSAC. A family that files by
  2 March and never confirms the GPA still gets nothing. California also has a
  second, later window (2 September) for community-college students.
- **"As soon as possible" is the honest answer in several states, not a missing
  date.** Alabama, Colorado, Georgia, Kentucky and the Kansas Comprehensive
  Grant publish no state date at all; the deadline is the college's, or the
  money simply runs out. Product copy must not fill these in with a guess.

- **IL 2026-27 state aid for undocumented students was struck down mid-cycle.**
  ISAC banner, fetched 2026-09-14: "On July 24, 2026, a federal district court
  ruled that undocumented students are no longer eligible to receive financial
  assistance from the Illinois Student Assistance Commission (ISAC) for the
  2026-2027 school year." The Illinois Alternative Application (the non-FAFSA
  route into MAP) "was closed on July 30, 2026 and no additional applications
  will be accepted for the 2026-27 school year." Any product copy that tells an
  Illinois student to use the Alternative Application is now wrong.
- **IL MAP has two different dates and neither is a hard deadline.** April 23,
  2026 is a _suspension_ date - later FAFSAs go on a waiting list. March 1, 2026
  is a _priority access_ date for returning recipients only, and ISAC says
  missing it "does not disqualify any applicant from receiving a grant if
  sufficient funding is available".
- **Secondary source conflicts with the agencies in two states.** A
  savingforcollege.com deadline table says Indiana is **April 1** and Kansas is
  **April 1**. Both are wrong against the agency pages: Indiana's own pages say
  **April 15** in three separate places, and the Kansas Board of Regents
  publishes **no** state-level FAFSA deadline for the Comprehensive Grant at all
  (it points students at each college's own priority deadline). The same table
  gives Louisiana as "June 30, 2028 / Feb 1, 2027 recommended", which matches
  neither the July 1 initial deadline nor the July 1 final deadline in the LOSFA
  rules.
- **Two Indiana "April 15" rules are different things.** The aid deadline ("File
  the FAFSA by the April 15th deadline each year") and the SEA 167-2023
  high-school graduation mandate (file the FAFSA or affirmatively opt out by
  April 15) share a date but not a purpose. Do not merge them.
- **Iowa's general guidance contradicts its own program pages.** The Apply for
  FAFSA page says state deadlines "fall as early as March 1"; the earliest date
  actually published on a program page is April 1 (All Iowa Opportunity
  Scholarship). The program pages win.
- **One Iowa deadline runs backwards from the rest.** Future Ready Iowa
  Last-Dollar is "June 30, 2027: FAFSA deadline for 2026-27 school year" - the
  END of the award year, while Iowa Tuition Grant, Kibbie, Voc-Tech and
  Workforce Grant are all July 1, 2026, before it starts.
- **Stale FAFSA open dates are everywhere.** Iowa Tuition Grant says "Oct. 1,
  2025: FAFSA is available for the 2026-27 academic year", KHEAA says "The FAFSA
  opens on October 1 each year", ISAC says "typically October 1", and Idaho says
  "once it opens on October 1". The 2026-27 FAFSA actually opened 24 September
  2025. The deadlines on those pages are still the agencies' own and were
  recorded; the open dates are not reliable.
- **The Louisiana source is a 2019 print of the administrative code.** The rules
  PDF is stamped "Louisiana Administrative Code April 2019". The July 1 rule has
  been stable since the 2007-2008 award year by its own text, and LOSFA still
  links the file, but a human confirming a TOPS date for a family should check
  for a newer LAC print.
- **Kentucky and Kansas Comprehensive Grant have no date to show a user.** Both
  are pure first-come / funds-limited programs. Product copy must not invent
  one; the honest instruction is "file as soon as the form opens".
