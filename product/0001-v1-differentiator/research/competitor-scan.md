# Competitive Landscape Scan: College-Planning / College-Counseling Apps (2024–2026)

**Purpose:** Ground the choice of a v1 differentiating feature for Unicoach
(paid, chat-centric AI college coach for high-school students, iOS). **Method:**
Direct fetches of vendor sites + secondary sources (Wikipedia, search snippets),
Feb 2026. All claims cited. **Note on sourcing:** Serper web search was
unavailable in this session; evidence comes from first-party pages fetched
directly (strongest kind for a feature/pricing scan) plus DuckDuckGo/Bing result
snippets.

---

## 1. Competitor profiles

### 1.1 Scoir — "The Scoir College Network"

- **What:** Unified network connecting students, guardians, counselors, and
  college admissions offices; college/career discovery, application management,
  Common App integration for the 2025–26 cycle; iOS mobile app. Claims "1 in 8
  college-bound students" are on the network.
  ([scoir.com](https://www.scoir.com/),
  [pricing](https://www.scoir.com/students-families/pricing))
- **Pricing / who pays:** Students, international students, parents/guardians:
  **$0** ("pay nothing… we do not sell or share your personal information").
  Revenue comes from **high schools/districts** (counselor advising suite,
  per-school plans) and **colleges** (recruitment plans "built around your
  enrollment goals").
  ([scoir.com/students-families/pricing](https://www.scoir.com/students-families/pricing),
  [scoir.com/blog — new college plans](https://www.scoir.com/blog/for-colleges-3-news-plans-built-around-your-enrollment-goals))
- **Strengths:** Counselor workflow + document rails, guardian accounts, privacy
  positioning, two-sided network with colleges.
- **Gap:** Not an advisor. No AI coach; guidance quality depends on the school's
  counselor.

### 1.2 Niche

- **What:** Rankings + reviews platform (formerly College Prowler) covering
  K-12, colleges, cities; college quiz, admissions calculator, scholarship
  listings ($2,000 no-essay sweepstakes), **Niche Direct Admissions®** ("more
  than 1 million students got accepted in 2026, average annual scholarship
  $17,500 — no essays, no rejections"), and **True Cost / Aid Offers / Funding
  Plan** tools.
  ([niche.com/about/direct-admissions](https://www.niche.com/about/direct-admissions/),
  [niche.com/truecost](https://www.niche.com/truecost/))
- **Pricing / who pays:** Free for students; **15,000+ school and college
  clients pay** for profiles/recruitment — a two-sided lead-gen model.
  ([Wikipedia: Niche (company)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niche_(company)))
- **Strengths:** Massive SEO gravity, the review corpus (authentic student
  voice), direct-admissions marketplace, and the most serious consumer attempt
  at cost transparency (True Cost).
- **Gap:** It's a media/marketplace site, not a coach. Guidance is static
  content; incentives tilt toward colleges who pay for enrollment.

### 1.3 Appily (EAB)

- **What:** "Free college planning platform… find, compare, and pay for college
  in one place": 4,000+ college profiles, ~250,000 scholarships, immersive
  virtual tours, **direct admissions offers**, acceptance calculator, college
  list maker, planner, major/vibe quizzes, parent hub, counselor hub.
  ([appily.com/about](https://www.appily.com/about),
  [appily.com](https://www.appily.com/))
- **Pricing / who pays:** Free to students; funded by EAB's college-side
  enrollment-marketing business (colleges pay for student leads and tours).
- **Strengths:** Breadth (search + scholarships + tours + direct admission in
  one place), EAB's college relationships.
- **Gap:** Commodity IPEDS-grade data dressed up; no persistent coaching
  relationship; monetization is college-side lead-gen.

### 1.4 CollegeVine — pivoted away from students

- **What (now):** "The **AI operating system for universities**… deploys AI
  agents to support operational excellence at **250+ institutions**" (IPEDS
  insights agent, CRM analyst, enrollment/advancement/finance agents). The
  consumer chancing engine and essay guides survive mostly as legacy blog/SEO.
  ([collegevine.com](https://www.collegevine.com/),
  [blog.collegevine.com](https://blog.collegevine.com/how-to-write-the-common-application-essays/))
- **Strategic signal:** The best-known free consumer chancing/advising platform
  **could not sustain a consumer business** and pivoted B2B — the consumer
  freemium advising market is monetization-hostile unless you sell to colleges
  or charge families for something they can't get elsewhere. Students found the
  chancing engine unreliable at the top end anyway
  ([r/ApplyingToCollege thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/ApplyingToCollege/comments/13358nn/how_accurate_was_collegevine_chances_cal)).

### 1.5 Empowerly

- **What:** Premium human counseling: 1:1 matched counselors, essay-editing team
  (48h turnaround), proprietary **Empowerly Score®** applicant-profile
  calculator, **Admissions Committee Review** by former admissions officers,
  plus paid add-ons (research scholar w/ PhD mentor, startup internships, AI
  Scholar, test prep, neurodivergent-student GOALS program). Claims "98% success
  rate." ([empowerly.com/pricing](https://empowerly.com/pricing/),
  [empowerly.com](https://empowerly.com/))
- **Pricing / who pays:** **Families pay; prices are quote-only**
  (book-a-consultation funnel). Third-party reviews describe it as a
  package-based consulting service in the typical IEC multi-thousand-dollar
  range
  ([Miami Herald review](https://www.miamiherald.com/careers-education/empowerly-review/)).
- **Strengths:** High-touch human judgment, accountability, outcome claims. This
  is what a $20/mo AI coach is the "10x cheaper" alternative to.

### 1.6 Naviance / PowerSchool CCLR

- **What:** The district-side incumbent: "college, career, and life readiness" —
  course planning, career assessments, scattergrams of past admits from your
  school, application/eDocs rails, parent portal. "Equipping **over 10 million
  students in 40% of U.S. high schools**."
  ([powerschool.com/solutions/naviance-campaign](https://www.powerschool.com/solutions/naviance-campaign/),
  [CCLR product page](https://www.powerschool.com/classroom/naviance/))
- **Pricing / who pays:** **Districts pay** per-student; students/parents get it
  via school login only.
- **Strengths:** Institutional lock-in, transcript/recommendation rails, the
  _only_ per-school historical-admissions scattergram data.
- **Gap:** Students famously tolerate it rather than love it; UX is a compliance
  tool; nothing proactive or conversational; unavailable to families whose
  school doesn't buy it.

### 1.7 MaiaLearning

- **What:** Naviance competitor for schools/districts: K-5 "Pathfinders" career
  exploration through high-school college applications, career readiness,
  micro-credentials, work-based learning, "MaiaLearning AI", GuidedPath edition
  for independent counselors (IECs), and a college-side recruitment product.
  ([maialearning.com](https://www.maialearning.com/))
- **Pricing / who pays:** School/district contracts (request-a-demo); IECs pay
  for GuidedPath.
- **Strengths:** Modern Naviance alternative; already marketing an AI layer to
  schools.

### 1.8 Common App

- **What:** The free application rails (1,000+ member colleges) plus planning
  content: college prep checklists, essay prompts, scholarship matching, "Next
  Chapter" direct-admissions initiative; mobile app for application tracking.
  ([commonapp.org/plan](https://www.commonapp.org/plan),
  [commonapp.org](https://www.commonapp.org/))
- **Who pays:** Nonprofit; member colleges fund it. Free to students.
- **Relevance:** Not a competitor for _coaching_, but it owns the moment of
  truth (submission) and is pushing direct admissions + checklists downmarket —
  further commoditizing "application logistics."

### 1.9 CollegeAI-type AI startups

- **CollegeAI (collegeai.com):** effectively moribund — the site's TLS
  certificate is expired (fetch fails with `certificate has expired`, observed
  Feb 2026). Cautionary tale for thin-wrapper college-match startups.
- **Kollegio AI ([kollegio.ai](https://kollegio.ai/)):** "Your AI college
  counselor," claims ~300K+ students; AI college matching ("thousands of data
  points"), essay review, scholarship finder, activity feedback, **direct
  admissions**, freemium; also selling to colleges and educators.
- **ESAI ([esai.ai](https://www.esai.ai/)):** "Brandi," a **voice AI college &
  career coach**; narrative/identity tools ("Identity Intelligence™") for essays
  and matching; paid tiers, school partnerships, parent-facing page.
- **Pattern:** Many entrants, all converging on the same feature set (match
  list + essay AI + scholarships + direct admissions), all built on commodity
  data. None has a data moat; churn among them is high (CollegeAI). This is
  exactly the "another AI chat app" trap.

### 1.10 Generic ChatGPT / Claude

- **What:** The real default competitor. Free–$20/mo; excellent at essay
  feedback, brainstorming, Q&A about the process, and increasingly at
  search-grounded college questions.
- **Structural weaknesses (verified by what they cannot do):**
  1. **Stale/unverified institution data** — net prices, deadlines, merit-aid
     policies, test policies change yearly; hallucinated numbers on a $100K
     decision are disqualifying.
  2. **No longitudinal state** — no durable multi-year plan, no proactive
     nudges, no deadline ownership.
  3. **No family layer** — parents can't see or share anything.
  4. **Essay-integrity risk** — Common App's fraud policy constrains substantive
     AI-generated application content, so "AI writes your essay" is a liability,
     not a feature.
- Every consumer competitor above is bolting on chat; none has made the _coach_
  (memory + plan + accountability) the product.

## 2. Synthesis

### (a) Table stakes — everyone has it

| Feature                                                      | Who has it                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searchable college database w/ filters (cost, major, scores) | Niche, Appily, Scoir, Naviance, Maia, Common App                                                                          |
| Match quiz / best-fit list generator                         | Niche, Appily, Kollegio, ESAI, (CollegeVine legacy), ChatGPT                                                              |
| Chancing / acceptance calculator                             | Niche, Appily, Kollegio, Empowerly (Score®), Naviance scattergrams                                                        |
| Scholarship search                                           | Niche, Appily (~250K), Kollegio, Common App                                                                               |
| Essay prompts/tips + AI essay feedback                       | Kollegio, ESAI, Empowerly (human), CollegeVine blog, ChatGPT                                                              |
| Checklists / deadline content                                | Common App, Appily planner, Naviance/Maia tasks                                                                           |
| **Direct admissions offers**                                 | Niche (1M+ accepted 2026), Appily, Common App Next Chapter, Kollegio — _rapidly becoming table stakes for free platforms_ |
| A chatbot                                                    | everyone, or imminently everyone                                                                                          |

### (b) Genuinely rare or absent

1. **Trustworthy cost truth.** Real net price _for your family_, merit-aid
   likelihood, aid-offer decoding and comparison, 4-year (not 1-year) cost
   projections, "what will this actually cost after aid" per school. Only
   Niche's True Cost/Aid Offers gestures at this, web-only and shallow; federal
   net-price calculators are scattered and stale. **No one owns "the app that
   tells you the real price."**
2. **Parent–student collaboration.** Scoir has free guardian accounts
   (read-mostly network membership); Naviance's parent portal is district-gated;
   Appily has a content-only "Parent Hub." **No consumer product makes the
   family a first-class shared workspace** (shared list, shared budget, divided
   tasks, visibility without surveillance).
3. **A proactive longitudinal coach.** Naviance/Maia assign counselor-driven
   tasks (compliance, not coaching); consumer apps are pull-only. Nothing
   consumer-side owns the multi-year arc from sophomore exploration → enrollment
   with memory and initiative.
4. **Hard-to-find institution data.** Admit rates _by major_,
   secondary-admission/weed-out risk for impacted majors, honors-college
   mechanics, merit-aid cutoffs, actual (not sticker) outcomes. Absent
   everywhere; even Naviance only has per-school history.

### (c) Where an AI-native coach with a proprietary institution dataset stands out

The market splits into (i) **free lead-gen marketplaces** paid by colleges
(Niche, Appily, Scoir, Common App) — structurally conflicted, guidance is
static; (ii) **district compliance software** (Naviance, Maia) — inaccessible to
many families, unloved by students; (iii) **expensive human consulting**
(Empowerly, IECs) — thousands of dollars; (iv) **thin AI wrappers** (Kollegio,
ESAI, dead CollegeAI) and **generic LLMs** — no data moat, no state, no trust. A
**family-paid AI coach grounded in a verified proprietary dataset** occupies the
empty quadrant: _aligned with the family (not colleges), affordable (vs. IECs),
and factually trustworthy (vs. ChatGPT)_. The dataset is what makes the chat
defensible: every answer the generic LLM gets wrong or can't source is the
product demo. CollegeVine's pivot is the proof that a consumer advising product
without either a data moat or family-side willingness to pay dies; the moat and
the payer must both be designed in.

### (d) Candidate-feature differentiation ranking

| Rank          | Candidate                                                | Verdict                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1 (most)**  | **C4 Cost/financial transparency**                       | Nearly empty space (only Niche True Cost, shallow). Highest family pain on a $100K+ decision; the payer (parents) directly values it; generic LLMs are _worst_ here (stale numbers = broken trust). Natural paywall: people pay $/mo to save $10K+.                                                                            |
| **2**         | **C1 Rich institution database incl. hard-to-find data** | Breadth is commodity (Niche/Appily already have 4,000+ profiles), but the _hard-to-find_ slice (admit-by-major, merit-aid odds, weed-out risk, true net cost) exists nowhere and is the grounding moat that makes the AI coach non-generic. Expensive to build broadly — build the differentiated slice, not the encyclopedia. |
| **3**         | **C5 Parent–student collaboration**                      | Genuinely rare in consumer apps and aimed straight at the subscriber (parents). Weaker as a _headline_ acquisition hook than C4, but the strongest retention/conversion lever.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **4**         | **C3 Todo/roadmap**                                      | Common in institutional tools (Naviance/Maia tasks, Appily planner, Common App checklists). A _proactive AI-owned_ roadmap is a real quality edge but reads as a feature, not a reason to download. Necessary scaffolding, not the differentiator.                                                                             |
| **5 (least)** | **C2 Top-schools list**                                  | Fully commoditized: every competitor and free ChatGPT produce match lists. As a headline feature it _guarantees_ the "another AI chat app" perception.                                                                                                                                                                         |

## 3. Recommendation

**Lead v1 with C4 (cost/financial transparency), powered by a C1-style
proprietary data slice, with C5 (parent collaboration) as the fast-follow
conversion layer.**

Concretely:

1. **Headline: "Know what college will actually cost you."** Per-school
   personalized net-price estimates, merit-aid likelihood, 4-year projections,
   and (in-season) aid-offer decoding/comparison — delivered conversationally by
   the coach and grounded in a curated, verified cost dataset. This is the one
   promise no incumbent owns, the one place ChatGPT reliably fails, and the one
   feature the _paying_ parent instantly values.
2. **Scope C1 as the cost-and-fit data moat, not an encyclopedia.** Don't
   compete with Niche/Appily on 4,000 generic profiles; own the hard-to-find
   fields (net price by income band, merit cutoffs, admit-by-major) for the
   ~300–500 schools that cover most real lists, and let the coach cite them.
3. **Ship C5 quickly after launch:** shared family workspace (list + budget +
   tasks) converts the parent from payer to daily user — no consumer competitor
   does this.
4. **Build C3 as invisible scaffolding** (the coach proactively drives the
   timeline) and treat **C2 as a table-stakes capability**, never the pitch.

**Positioning sentence:** _"Every other app helps colleges find students.
Unicoach is the coach your family pays — so it tells you the truth, starting
with the price."_
